What JEE Advanced Toppers Did Differently

Nineteen real toppers share the study habits, schedules and exam-day tactics that helped them crack the JEE Advanced.

AIR 1
VL
Ved Lahoti

Tip: Create a disciplined study schedule and revise core concepts multiple times.

AIR 2
AD
Aditya

Tip: Prioritise understanding over rote learning; practice concept application.

AIR 3
BS
Bhogalapalli Sandesh

Tip: Solve previous years' papers to recognise patterns and question styles.

AIR 4
RK
Rhythm Kedia

Tip: Take timed mock tests to improve speed and accuracy under pressure.

AIR 5
PK
Putti Kushal Kumar

Tip: Maintain short notes for last-minute revisions and quick recall.

AIR 6
RM
Rajdeep Mishra

Tip: Analyse every mock test error and correct the underlying conceptual gap.

AIR 7
DP
Dwija Dharmeshkumar Patel

Tip: Balance all subjects; give weak areas targeted daily practice.

AIR 8
KT
Koduru Tejeswar

Tip: Keep your study space tidy and distraction-free for focused sessions.

AIR 9
DD
Dhruvin Hemant Doshi

Tip: Simulate exam conditions for practice tests to build stamina and focus.

AIR 10
AS
Alladaboina S S D B Sidhvik Suhas

Tip: Revise formulas and key derivations daily for quick recall during exams.

AIR 11
PB
Parth Bawankule

Tip: Mark difficult problems and revisit them regularly to reduce fear of tough questions.

AIR 12
RS
Raghav Sharma

Tip: Break large topics into smaller tasks; small wins keep motivation high.

AIR 13
MB
Matcha Balaaditya

Tip: Use error logs to track recurring mistakes and focus revisions there.

AIR 14
BB
Bibaswan Biswas

Tip: Teach tough concepts to a peer or to yourself — teaching reveals gaps fast.

AIR 15
SM
Sumukh M G

Tip: Keep mock-test review sessions longer than the test itself to deeply analyse performance.

AIR 16
CS
Chunchikala Sreecharan

Tip: Trust a small set of good-quality books instead of flipping many resources.

AIR 17
GJ
Gunda Joshmitha

Tip: Maintain consistent sleep and short exercise breaks to keep the mind fresh.

AIR 18
HG
Hemant Godve

Tip: Practice mixed-topic question sets to learn switching between concepts fast.

AIR 19
SS
Sangye Norphel Sherpa

Tip: Avoid last-minute new topics; focus on consolidation and accuracy instead.
